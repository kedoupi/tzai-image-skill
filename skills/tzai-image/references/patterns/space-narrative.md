# Space And Narrative

## `architecture-space`
State program, site, scale, circulation, material, daylight, and viewpoint. Use `architecture` for a concept diagram and `photo` or `landscape` for experiential views. Label all outputs as conceptual; architects must validate buildability, codes, dimensions, and safety.

## `scene-storytelling`
Write one beat: who acts, what changes, where it occurs, and what the viewer should feel. Choose a camera framing that clarifies action. Keep dialogue and context as external captions.
