# Character And World

## `character-sheet`
Define role, silhouette, proportions, palette, wardrobe, expressions, and usage context. Approve a master pose before variations. Use original traits and avoid resemblance to protected characters or harmful caricature.

## `collectible-toy`
Confirm character ownership, intended collector audience, materials, pose, and packaging context. Generate with `mascot` or `product-photo` for concept exploration. Product engineering, marks, warnings, and safety claims require separate review.
