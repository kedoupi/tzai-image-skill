# Photography And Illustration

## `ink-double-exposure`
Pair one approved subject silhouette with one secondary motif. Use restrained ink, contrast, and overlap so both ideas remain readable. It is an editorial interpretation, not evidence.

## `nature-science`
Anchor species, habitat, season, and scientific facts to supplied sources. Use `photo`, `landscape`, or `illustration` according to interpretive need. Review anatomy, scale, and environmental claims before delivery.

## `realistic-photo`
State subject, setting, camera distance, light, and intended use. Use a fictional or consented subject. Mark synthetic editorial scenes clearly when they could be mistaken for factual photography.

## `documentary-moment`
Describe an observed-feeling human action, environment, and emotional distance without claiming an event occurred. Secure consent for identifiable references. Deliver as illustrative reconstruction unless provenance proves otherwise.

## `illustration-style`
Define repeatable rules for shape language, palette, line weight, texture, perspective, and motif. Approve one anchor before the series. Describe qualities rather than requesting a living artist's style.
