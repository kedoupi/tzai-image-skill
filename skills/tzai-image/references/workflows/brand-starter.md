# Brand Starter

Use for requests such as "create a brand identity direction," "explore a logo system," or "make a visual starter kit." Produce 3-6 exploratory identity assets, not a registered or production-ready brand system.

## Intake

Infer the offer, audience, personality, usage contexts, and existing visual constraints. Ask only for material gaps: brand name, intended market, prohibited similarities, existing assets, or primary use case. Record that ownership and clearance remain the user's responsibility.

## Plan And Approval

Propose one coherent direction before paid calls:

| Asset | Role | Core direction | Kind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Identity board | Palette, material, typography mood | `moodboard` |
| 02 | Primary mark direction | Distinctive symbol/wordmark cue | `logo` |
| 03-N | Application direction | App mark or alternate mark | `icon` or `logo` |

State the name treatment, palette notes, visual principles, and prohibited references. Choose exactly one identity-board anchor and obtain explicit plan approval.

## Two-Stage Production

1. Generate exactly one `moodboard` identity-board anchor.
2. Obtain explicit anchor approval before remaining paid assets.
3. Generate the planned `logo` and `icon` directions with `--ref` to the approved board, preserving its palette and material language.

## Delivery

Return 3-6 numbered files, an identity-board summary, mark-direction notes, palette notes, and intended touchpoint for each asset.

## Quality And Boundaries

- Treat rendered lettering as exploratory; final vector marks, typography, color specifications, and accessibility contrast require design production.
- If one asset fails, preserve approved files and regenerate only that asset from the anchor; report partial delivery clearly.
- Do not imitate a living artist or competitor, assert trademark availability, or present generated marks as cleared, exclusive, or legally registrable.
