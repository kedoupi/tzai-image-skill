# WeChat Article

Use for requests such as "turn this into a WeChat article," "make a WeChat post with a cover," or "package this article for publishing." Produce 1-8 assets: one header visual and up to seven section visuals.

## Intake

Read the supplied article and infer audience, purpose, heading structure, brand voice, and visual opportunities. Ask only for material gaps: source text, verified facts/citations, mandatory brand rules, or publishing CTA. Do not silently rewrite unsupported facts.

## Plan And Approval

Prepare `article.md` plus an approved visual map before paid calls:

| Placement | Heading/purpose | Content point | Visual approach | Kind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Header | Article promise | Opening idea | Header visual | `wechat` |
| Optional sections | Specific heading | One verified point | Illustration or explanation | `illustration` or `infographic` |
| Optional share asset | Distribution hook | Short promise | Editorial cover | `cover` |

`article.md` contains title, body, headings, citations or source placeholders, CTA, and image insertion markers. Name exactly one header visual as the anchor and obtain explicit plan approval.

## Two-Stage Production

1. Generate exactly one `wechat` header anchor.
2. Obtain explicit anchor approval before remaining paid assets.
3. Create approved optional `illustration`, `infographic`, or `cover` assets with `--ref` to the anchor. Match the article's content plan, not merely its keywords.

## Delivery

Return `article.md`, the header file, optional numbered section files, and a placement map with filename, heading, caption, and alt text. Include only the approved 1-8 assets.

## Quality And Boundaries

- Keep detailed labels, citations, and final Chinese copy in `article.md`; check rendered text for fidelity and regenerate poor text.
- Preserve completed assets if one fails; report its placement and resume only after the corrected asset is approved when it affects the series.
- This is a publishing package, not fact checking, legal review, formatting inside WeChat, or publication.
