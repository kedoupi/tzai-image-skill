# Speculative Design
## Intake
Collect future horizon, audience, intended provocation, assumptions, and prohibited claims.
## Plan
Define one scenario and 2-6 concept views labeled as speculative.
## Approval/anchor
Approve assumptions and label; generate and approve the concept anchor.
## Production
Create `illustration`, `ui`, `architecture`, or `cover` concept assets.
## Deliverables
Return concept views, assumptions, and speculation label.
