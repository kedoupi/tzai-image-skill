# Character IP

Use for requests such as "create a mascot IP," "design an original character," or "make character poses and scenes." Produce 3-8 original starter assets.

## Intake

Infer intended audience, role, personality, setting, usage, and visual tone. Ask only for material gaps: ownership, prohibited similarities, primary channel, character traits, or whether a scene or toy concept is needed. Reject requests to copy a recognizable protected character or living artist's signature style.

## Plan And Approval

Present a character bible and asset plan before paid calls:

| Asset | Purpose | Character constraint | Kind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Character sheet | Silhouette, palette, outfit, expressions | `mascot` |
| 02-N | Pose, scene, story, or toy concept | Preserve defining traits | `illustration`, `storybook`, `mascot`, or `product-photo` |

Define silhouette, palette, wardrobe, expression range, proportions, and prohibited similarities. Select exactly one character-sheet anchor and obtain explicit plan approval.

## Two-Stage Production

1. Generate exactly one `mascot` character-sheet anchor.
2. Obtain explicit anchor approval before producing any remaining paid assets.
3. Generate approved poses and scenes with their planned existing kinds and `--ref` to the anchor. Preserve recognizability across every asset.

## Delivery

Return 3-8 numbered files, the character bible, a pose/expression list, and scene or toy-concept notes where requested.

## Quality And Boundaries

- Treat rendered names and small costume labels as drafts; regenerate inaccurate text and keep final text outside the image.
- On partial failure, retain completed assets and regenerate only the affected role from the approved anchor.
- This is original character exploration, not ownership transfer, IP clearance, merchandising specification, or a license to use third-party franchises.
