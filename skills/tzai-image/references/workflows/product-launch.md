# Product Launch

Use for requests such as "prepare product launch imagery," "make launch visuals," or "create launch channel assets." Produce 3-8 coordinated assets around a verified product.

## Intake

Infer the product, launch objective, audience, channels, and primary promise from approved materials. Ask only for material gaps: accurate product references, approved claims, dates/prices, channel formats, mandatory legal copy, or brand rules. Do not fabricate product capabilities or physical details.

## Plan And Approval

Create the launch content and asset plan before paid calls:

| Asset | Channel/role | Approved message | Copy-safe area | Kind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Launch hero | Primary promise | Reserved area | `product-photo` |
| 02-N | Campaign/channel variant | One approved message | Reserved area | `banner`, `cover`, or `email-header` |

Specify the visual system, product angle, and use of supplied references. Select exactly one launch hero anchor and obtain explicit plan approval.

## Two-Stage Production

1. Generate exactly one `product-photo` launch-hero anchor, using verified product references where available.
2. Obtain explicit anchor approval before any remaining paid assets.
3. Generate planned `banner`, `cover`, `email-header`, or additional `product-photo` files with `--ref` to the anchor. Preserve product attributes and leave approved copy-safe space.

## Delivery

Return 3-8 numbered files and a usage map with channel, message, safe area, source product reference, and final-copy owner.

## Quality And Boundaries

- Final prices, offers, legal copy, and small text must be typeset externally and approved; regenerate inaccurate product depictions.
- On partial failure, deliver successful files, identify the missing role, and regenerate only it from the approved anchor.
- This is marketing visualization, not product photography evidence, claim substantiation, rights clearance, or regulatory approval.
