# XHS Note

Use for requests such as "make a Xiaohongshu note," "turn this article into XHS cards," or "create a five-card social post." Produce 2-10 coherent cards plus publishable note copy.

## Intake

Infer topic, audience, desired action, source claims, and an appropriate card count from supplied content. Ask only for material gaps: verified claim/source, brand restrictions, card count when it changes the narrative, or CTA. Do not invent endorsements, results, prices, or regulated claims.

## Plan And Approval

Draft `note.md` and a storyboard before paid calls:

| Card | Role | On-card message | Visual direction | Kind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cover hook | Short title | Series anchor | `xhs-cover` |
| 2-N | Insight, step, proof, or close | One point per card | Shared system | `xhs` |

`note.md` contains title, body, hashtags, and CTA. Keep image text to a short hook; retain exact copy in `note.md`. Specify the shared style, layout, palette, and safe text areas. Obtain explicit plan approval.

## Two-Stage Production

1. Generate exactly one cover-card anchor with `xhs-cover`.
2. Obtain explicit anchor approval before producing any other card.
3. Generate cards 2-N with `xhs` and `--ref` to the approved cover. Preserve palette, hierarchy, subject treatment, and whitespace while varying the idea per card.

## Delivery

Deliver `note.md`, numbered card files, and the card storyboard linking every file to its message and placement. The asset map must identify the cover and all cards in order.

## Quality And Boundaries

- Verify all factual claims and hashtags; final caption copy is authoritative over rendered text.
- Regenerate a card with wrong or unreadable text rather than retouching it. On partial failure, return completed files, the failed card role, and the next retry target.
- This prepares social content only; it does not publish, guarantee platform performance, or substitute legal, medical, financial, or advertising review.
