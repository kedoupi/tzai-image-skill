# Campaign Kit

Use for requests such as "build a campaign asset kit," "make event campaign visuals," or "create multi-channel creative." Produce 3-10 reusable campaign assets.

## Intake

Infer objective, audience, campaign message, channels, timing, and brand system from the brief. Ask only for material gaps: approved offer/claim, required formats, supplied brand assets, required CTA, or rights restrictions. Flag unverified promotions, partner marks, and licensed imagery.

## Plan And Approval

Create a content and format matrix before paid calls:

| Asset | Channel/role | Message | Copy-safe zone | Kind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Key visual | Campaign idea | Primary title area | `poster` |
| 02-N | Distribution variant | One channel message | Format-specific area | `banner`, `email-header`, `cover`, or `poster` |

Define the shared palette, visual motif, title placement, and CTA treatment. Identify exactly one key visual as anchor and obtain explicit plan approval.

## Two-Stage Production

1. Generate exactly one `poster` key-visual anchor.
2. Obtain explicit anchor approval before producing remaining paid assets.
3. Generate the approved variants with their planned existing kinds and `--ref` to the key visual; adapt composition for each channel without changing the campaign idea.

## Delivery

Return 3-10 numbered files, a format-variant matrix, a copy-safe map, and the approved message assigned to each asset.

## Quality And Boundaries

- Use short rendered hooks only; final headlines, dates, terms, CTAs, and partner marks require external typesetting and verification.
- Preserve completed assets if a variant fails; regenerate only that role from the approved anchor and report the gap.
- Do not imply endorsements, rights, offer validity, or campaign performance.
