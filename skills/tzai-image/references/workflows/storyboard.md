# Storyboard
## Intake
Collect story beat, audience, characters, setting, tone, and frame count.
## Plan
Write 3-12 ordered beats with camera framing and transition purpose.
## Approval/anchor
Approve the beat sheet; generate and approve the opening-frame anchor.
## Production
Create sequential `illustration`, `storybook`, or `photo` frames.
## Deliverables
Return ordered frames and beat sheet.
