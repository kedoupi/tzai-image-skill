# Photography Direction
## Intake
Collect subject consent, usage, setting, mood, references, and must-not-show items.
## Plan
Define lighting, framing, wardrobe or props, and 2-6 shot roles.
## Approval/anchor
Approve direction; generate and approve a mood-anchor image.
## Production
Create `photo`, `landscape`, `food`, or `product-photo` direction images.
## Deliverables
Return direction board, shot list, and rights notes.
