# Article Illustration

Use for requests such as "add visuals to this article," "illustrate these chapters," or "propose section images before generating." Produce 2-8 coordinated visuals, including a cover or first-section anchor.

## Intake And Analysis

Read the real supplied title and headings; do not invent a generic chapter structure. Infer audience, argument, tone, and source-backed facts. Ask only for material gaps: article source, brand rules, facts that affect the visual, or an intended illustration count.

For every heading, decide whether an image improves comprehension, pacing, or hierarchy. Do not propose decoration-only images. Prefer `cover` for a hero, `illustration` for a concept, `infographic` for comparisons or metrics, and `diagram` for relationships.

## Plan And Approval

Present an illustration plan before paid calls:

| File | Real heading | Why it needs a visual | Kind | Caption | Alt text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Title or first selected heading | Establish visual language | `cover` or `illustration` | Draft | Draft |
| 02-N | Exact selected heading | Explain a specific point | Existing kind | Draft | Draft |

Include a shared style token and identify exactly one anchor. Obtain explicit plan approval before paid generation.

## Two-Stage Production

1. Generate exactly one cover or first-illustration anchor using its planned kind.
2. Obtain explicit anchor approval before generating any remaining assets.
3. Produce the other approved visuals with their planned existing kinds and `--ref` to the anchor. Preserve the argument and style; do not force every heading into an image.

## Delivery

Return numbered image files and the final illustration plan containing each real heading, insertion point, caption, and alt text. Provide 2-8 assets only.

## Quality And Boundaries

- Verify factual diagrams, quantities, names, and labels against the source. Place dense labels and final copy in article layout, not the generated image.
- Regenerate only a failed or text-inaccurate image using the approved anchor; retain successful files and disclose partial results.
- This supplies editorial visuals, not article authorship, source validation, accessibility conformance, or legal clearance.
